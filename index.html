MATEAR — Moon Adaptive Technology for Extraterrestrial Architectural Robotics

A research project developing robotic systems and construction methods for the Moon. It studies how autonomous and semi-autonomous machines can use in-situ resources like lunar regolith to build landing pads, habitats and surface stabilization. The repository holds the experimental procedures, datasets, results and analysis.

Duckweed Labs — plant-growth instrumentation

A sensor and human-machine-interface system for monitoring duckweed cultures. Work includes validating the full sensor suite, building remote access to live data over a private network, and a notebook that estimates duckweed surface area from images. Matedry — wireless weather station with fan control

A battery-powered device built around an STM32 "Blue Pill" that reads temperature and humidity (DHT11) and drives a 24V fan through a MOSFET. A Bluetooth module (HC-05 or BLE HM-10) connects it to a companion mobile app. I designed the full power chain: USB-C charging (TP5100), a protected 2S Li-ion pack (BMS), a 5V buck for the logic and a 24V boost for the fan. The repo has firmware for the fan controller and weather station, wiring and component docs, a flashing guide, and a no-battery variant.
